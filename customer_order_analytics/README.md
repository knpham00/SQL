# Customer & Order Analytics

## Overview
Analyzed customer purchasing behavior and order performance across 
January and February sales data using PostgreSQL. Focused on revenue 
trends, product performance, and customer segmentation.

## Dataset
- Source: BIT_DB (Break Into Tech course database)
- Tables: JanSales, FebSales, customers
- Multi-table relational database with order and customer data

## Business Questions Answered
1. How many orders were placed in January vs February?
2. Which products generated the most revenue?
3. Which locations had the highest order volume?
4. How do customers segment by spending behavior?
5. What was the average spend per account?

## SQL Concepts Used
JOINs | Aggregations | GROUP BY | HAVING | Subqueries | 
CTEs | Window Functions | CASE Statements | Filtering

## Key Findings
- The MacBook Pro Laptop was the top revenue driver in January
- High-value customers (spending $1,000+) represent a small but 
  significant segment of February buyers
- Los Angeles had the highest product volume of any single market
